# QUINTUM Community

QUINTUM is currently a **solo-founder, public-Testnet project**. The community starts from zero and is being built in public around real testing, mining, code review and documentation rather than promises of profit.

## First objective: 10 real participants

The first community milestone is not follower count. It is:

- 3 independent people who install the Windows client;
- 3 independent people who run or help run nodes;
- 2 people who mine Testnet blocks;
- 2 people who send and receive real Testnet transactions;
- at least 5 useful bug reports, test reports or documentation improvements;
- at least 1 external code/documentation contribution if someone is willing.

One person may cover more than one role.

## How to participate

1. Download the official public Testnet release:
   https://github.com/Sheff1981/quintum-core/releases/tag/v0.0.1-prealpha
2. Verify `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
3. Install QUINTUM Core.
4. Let the wallet discover peers and synchronize automatically.
5. Test receiving, sending, mining, restart, backup and recovery behavior.
6. Report reproducible problems through GitHub Issues.
7. Developers may submit narrowly scoped pull requests under the MIT license.

## Community principles

- No fake users, fake followers or fake activity.
- No paid claims represented as organic support.
- No promise that Testnet coins will become Mainnet coins.
- No promise of profit, price or exchange listing.
- No hidden premine or privileged developer mint.
- Testnet QMU has no monetary value.
- Mainnet launch will be announced in advance if/when the network reaches the required technical and security readiness.
- Mainnet participation should be open and verifiable, with final binaries, source, hashes, network parameters and launch time published before launch.

## What helps most right now

The highest-value contribution is **running the software and reporting what actually happens**.

Useful reports include:

- installer failures;
- first-start problems;
- peer discovery failures;
- synchronization stalls;
- wallet creation/restore problems;
- incorrect balances;
- mining stops or incorrect hash-rate display;
- transaction relay/confirmation problems;
- restart/persistence problems;
- backup/restore failures;
- UI inconsistencies;
- reproducible crashes.

## Fair-launch direction

QUINTUM is being developed toward a fair-launch model inspired by early native PoW networks:

1. public source and Testnet;
2. public release candidates;
3. advance Mainnet launch notice;
4. final Genesis/network parameters published before launch;
5. no hidden creator premine;
6. permissionless mining from Mainnet launch;
7. price, if one later exists, emerges from real market participants rather than being hard-coded by the protocol.

This is a development direction, not a promise of future monetary value.
